Platform Insights & Recommendations

Meta Strong performer
  • Meta delivered the strongest conversion efficiency of all three platforms at $15.78 cost per conversion — well below the blended average of $43.90 — making it the most cost-effective channel for direct response in this period.
  • Both ad sets performed at near-identical CPAs ($15.57 vs $16.46), suggesting the audience and creative approach is working consistently across both conversion goals. This is a healthy signal.
  • Notably, "How to get AML compliant before the deadline" was the strongest converting creative in the period with 7 conversions at the lowest CPA, but was paused during the flight. Given its performance, reactivating this creative is recommended — it may have been paused prematurely before the data had a chance to mature.
  • The Book a Demo ad set drove the majority of volume (130 of 169 conversions) on a significantly larger impression share, while Register performed efficiently on a smaller budget.
  • Recommendation Reactivate "How to get AML compliant before the deadline" across both Meta and LinkedIn. It was the top converting creative in this period and its pause may have limited overall performance. Running it alongside the current active creatives will allow a cleaner like-for-like comparison with sufficient data.
  • Recommendation Given the strong CPA and consistent performance, Meta warrants increased budget allocation in the next phase. Testing broader or lookalike audience expansion could help scale volume without significantly impacting efficiency.
  • Recommendation Consider introducing a third ad set to test a new creative angle or audience segment — the current stable baseline makes this a low-risk moment to experiment.
The Trade Desk Scale & reach
  • Trade Desk drove the bulk of overall reach and conversion volume — 2.58M impressions and 1,011 conversions — at an efficient blended CPM of $16.71, demonstrating strong value for broad audience coverage across professional segments.
  • The Display REA & Legal line item was the standout performer, delivering 546 conversions at a CPM of just $2.40 — significantly more cost-efficient than any other TTD line item and a strong argument for continued investment in this placement.
  • Within the Display REA & Legal line item, a newer fourth creative ("It's time to get clear") has had limited delivery relative to the two established creatives. This is expected given platform optimisation bias toward proven performers — it has not had a fair opportunity to demonstrate its potential and warrants being separated into its own ad group to allow independent testing.
  • PDOOH delivered 293,952 impressions across outdoor and office environments and while direct conversion tracking isn't available for this format, it plays an important role in brand awareness and supporting upper-funnel recognition among the real estate and legal audience.
  • BVOD and Audio showed higher CPMs as expected for premium video and audio inventory, with strong completion rates — indicating engaged audiences even where click-through is naturally lower for these formats.
  • Recommendation Prioritise budget toward the Display REA & Legal line item in the next flight given its clear efficiency advantage. The REA-PG display line also performed well and merits continued investment.
  • Recommendation Separate the newer "It's time to get clear" creative into its own ad group to give it a fair run against the existing performers without being suppressed by the platform's bias toward already-optimised creatives. Messages 2 and 3 are closely aligned in messaging and can continue running together.
  • Recommendation As campaign tracking matures, consider implementing a view-through attribution window for PDOOH to better capture its contribution to the overall conversion path.
LinkedIn Optimisation needed
  • LinkedIn delivered the highest CPM ($61.77) and cost per conversion ($302.17) of the three platforms, which is consistent with LinkedIn's premium pricing for professional audiences. However, the conversion volume of 23 over the period suggests the current setup has room to improve.
  • The Register ad set in particular returned a CPA of $557.63 on just 5 conversions — a signal that either the audience targeting, creative messaging, or landing page experience may not be optimally aligned for this conversion goal on LinkedIn.
  • LinkedIn's strength is in reaching senior decision-makers and verified professional personas that other platforms cannot accurately target. The higher cost per conversion should be weighed against the quality of the audience being reached, particularly for a product like PEXA Clear targeting real estate and legal professionals.
  • "How to get AML compliant before the deadline" was paused during the flight despite being the best performing Book a Demo creative by CPA ($196.65 vs $300.90 for the next best). This is worth revisiting — the data suggests it was resonating with the audience and deserves a full run to validate its efficiency.
  • Recommendation Reactivate "How to get AML compliant before the deadline" on LinkedIn. Given it was outperforming the other Book a Demo creatives on CPA before being paused, it should be given a full flight to assess its true potential.
  • Recommendation Review the creative and copy for the Register ad set specifically — LinkedIn audiences typically respond better to thought leadership framing and value-led messaging than direct response calls to action.
  • Recommendation Consider consolidating budget into the Book a Demo objective only for the next flight to improve efficiency, and revisit the Register goal once messaging has been refined.
  • Recommendation Test a Lead Gen Form format as an alternative to landing page conversions — LinkedIn's native form product typically reduces friction and improves conversion rates for professional audiences.
Methodology Notes
  • Period: Monday 20 April to Monday 4 May 2026 inclusive (15 days).
  • Meta: pulled at ad-set level for campaign "NB_BOF_Conversion_Apr_2026 — Book a Demo & Register".
  • LinkedIn: filtered to BOF/Conversions ad sets within NB_BOF_Acquisition_Apr_26 (excludes paused MOF Traffic ad sets).
  • The Trade Desk: account Bind Digital AUD. PEXA does not use BOF labelling on TTD — all delivering line items in the window are included.
  • Currency: AUD throughout. Spend reflects platform-reported costs (Meta: cost; LinkedIn: total spent; TTD: advertiser cost in adv currency).
  • Conversions: Meta = website conversions; LinkedIn = total conversions; TTD = total conversion 01.
  • PDOOH conversions are not directly trackable via TTD pixel — view-through attribution is excluded from conversion totals.
  • Timezone: Trade Desk figures use Australia/Sydney. Meta and LinkedIn use UTC (platform default).

Meta Strong performer
  • Meta continued to deliver the strongest conversion efficiency across all three platforms at $18.18 cost per conversion. It is worth noting that Meta figures include modelled conversions, where Meta estimates conversions it cannot directly observe due to privacy restrictions. We recommend validating these against your internal reporting — if the performance is consistent with what you are seeing, Meta is a strong case for continued budget investment.
  • The Book a Demo ad set drove the majority of volume (237 of 356 conversions) at $18.90 CPA. The Register ad set also performed efficiently at $16.73 CPA across 119 conversions.
  • "How to get AML compliant before the deadline" was the strongest performing creative in the period with 56 conversions at $15.76 CPA. Based on this performance, we are reactivating this message across all channels to give it a full run.
  • Action taken $5,011 of budget was moved from LinkedIn to Meta on Tuesday 19 May, as requested, given Meta's strong conversion performance relative to LinkedIn.
  • Action taken "How to get AML compliant before the deadline" reactivated across all channels as the best performing creative in the period.
  • Recommendation Given Meta's consistent efficiency, we would recommend considering further budget reallocation from LinkedIn to Meta in the next flight if internal data validates the conversion figures we are seeing in platform.
The Trade Desk Scale & reach
  • Trade Desk Display delivered strong reach over the 30-day period, generating 3.45M impressions and 453 conversions at a blended CPM of $5.07, demonstrating efficient broad coverage across real estate and legal professional audiences.
  • The REA and Legal line item drove the majority of volume at 318 conversions and a CPM of $2.70, making it the most cost-efficient line item in the buy. The REA-PG programmatic guaranteed line delivered 133 conversions at a higher CPM of $17.38, reflecting the premium nature of that inventory.
  • Action taken A new Register campaign on Biddable Display has been set up to separate messaging by conversion goal, with a 70/30 budget split applied between Book a Demo and Register.
  • Action taken PDOOH was switched off on Thursday. Remaining PDOOH budget was redirected to the Melbourne Pop Up event.
  • Action taken "How to get AML compliant before the deadline" reactivated across all channels as the best performing creative in the period.
  • Recommendation Monitor the new Biddable Display Register campaign closely in the first week to assess whether the 70/30 split is delivering efficient CPAs across both conversion goals, and adjust accordingly.
LinkedIn Optimisation needed
  • LinkedIn delivered 33 conversions over the 30-day period at a blended CPA of $423.24, consistent with LinkedIn's premium pricing for verified professional audiences. The cost per conversion reflects the platform's strength in audience quality rather than volume.
  • LinkedIn's value lies in accessing senior decision-makers in real estate and legal that other platforms cannot accurately target. The higher cost per conversion should be considered in the context of audience intent and downstream deal value rather than compared directly to Meta or Display benchmarks.
  • The Book a Demo ad set returned a CPA of $395.59 across 24 conversions. The Register ad set delivered 9 conversions at $496.99 CPA.
  • "How to get AML compliant before the deadline" was the most efficient creative at $196.65 CPA, reinforcing the case for its reactivation which has now been actioned across all channels.
  • Action taken $5,011 of LinkedIn budget was moved to Meta on Tuesday 19 May, as requested, to capitalise on Meta's stronger conversion performance.
  • Action taken "How to get AML compliant before the deadline" reactivated across all channels as the best performing creative in the period.
  • Recommendation If Meta's conversion performance is validated against your internal data, we would recommend continuing to shift budget from LinkedIn to Meta. Meta is delivering conversions at a significantly lower CPA and increasing its share of budget could meaningfully improve overall campaign efficiency.
  • Recommendation Consider testing a Lead Gen Form format as an alternative to landing page conversions. LinkedIn's native form product reduces friction and typically improves conversion rates for professional audiences.

Pop-Up Events - Findings Action required
  • Melbourne is materially outperforming Victoria on every efficiency metric. Melbourne is converting clicks at roughly 5-6x the CTR of Victoria (2.33% vs 0.40%) and 1/11th the CPC ($3.39 vs $38.11). The same three creative concepts are running across both events, so this is an audience or auction dynamic, not a creative problem.
  • Victoria spend is ~5x Melbourne's ($1,906 vs $359) despite worse efficiency. This looks like a budget pacing setting on the Victoria campaign group rather than an active optimisation choice and should be reviewed before the next flight.
  • Both campaigns are showing 0 conversions despite 156 landing page clicks. Likely the conversion pixel for the events isn't firing, or registrations are being tracked off-LinkedIn. To confirm before the next reporting cycle.
  • The "Is your path to compliance Clear?" creative is the strongest performer on Melbourne (3.52% CTR, $2.48 CPC) but the weakest on Victoria (0.33% CTR, $45.71 CPC). The Melbourne creative ranking is the inverse of Victoria's, which further supports that the audience/auction context is the dominant variable here.
  • Message Ads (Sponsored InMail) delivered a blended open rate of 26.88% (372 sends, 100 opens), slightly below LinkedIn's ~30% benchmark. Geelong opens (27.36%) are running ahead of Melbourne (24.07%), though the Melbourne sample (54 sends) is small.
  • Action required Check with Bind whether the event conversion pixel is firing correctly, or confirm whether registrations are being tracked through a different system.
  • Recommendation Rebalance budget toward Melbourne for any remaining flight - the efficiency gap is too large to ignore. If Victoria is being held at higher spend for reach or attendance reasons, that should be made explicit.
  • Recommendation Pause "Is your path to compliance Clear?" on Victoria and concentrate Victoria spend on "Join us at the PEXA Clear Pop-Up - Register today" which is the strongest Victoria creative.
Methodology Notes
  • Period: Monday 20 April to Tuesday 19 May 2026 inclusive (30 days).
  • Meta: pulled at ad-set level for campaign "NB_BOF_Conversion_Apr_2026 — Book a Demo & Register".
  • LinkedIn: filtered to BOF/Conversions ad sets within NB_BOF_Acquisition_Apr_26 (excludes paused MOF Traffic ad sets).
  • The Trade Desk: account Bind Digital AUD. PEXA does not use BOF labelling on TTD — all delivering Display line items in the window are included. BVOD and Audio excluded.
  • Currency: AUD throughout. Spend reflects platform-reported costs (Meta: cost; LinkedIn: total spent; TTD: advertiser cost in adv currency).
  • Conversions: Meta = website conversions; LinkedIn = total conversions; TTD = pixel events (Book a Demo Button Click + Completed; Register of Interest + Button Click + Successful Registration).
  • PDOOH conversions are not directly trackable via TTD pixel — view-through attribution is excluded from conversion totals.
  • Timezone: Trade Desk figures use Australia/Sydney. Meta and LinkedIn use UTC (platform default).
  • Pop-Up Events (Sponsored Content): account PEXA Clear (518688193), period 20 Apr - 19 May 2026. Campaign groups NB_BOF_Conversions_Event_Victoria_May_26 and NB_BOF_Conversions_Event_Melbourne_M, sub-campaigns "Website conversions - May 6, 2026".
  • Pop-Up Events (Message Ads): creative type SPONSORED_INMAILS (LinkedIn Message Ads, formerly Sponsored InMail). Sub-campaigns "Website conversions - May 7, 2026" (Victoria) and "Website conversions - May 14, 2026" (Melbourne).
